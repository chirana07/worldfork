# Comparison lab

Mode: Operate. The introduction explains why two agents face the same cancellation and how each booking affects the travelers still waiting. Replay opens with final scores and a shortcut to the first confirmed booking, with links to the ledger and archive. Playback sits above the paired worlds, whose seats and bookings reflect the selected logical tick. The live view offers a direct comparison action and puts advanced controls under Experiment settings. The interface stacks the worlds on small screens. The simulator and scoring rules determine all results.
