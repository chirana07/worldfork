# Comparison lab

Mode: Operate. Primary live action: launch two runs from the same initial world. Primary replay action: inspect a recorded comparison. Replay opens with final scores and links to the decision ledger and archive. Playback sits above the paired worlds, whose seats and bookings reflect the selected logical tick. Ledger receipts expose the actual recorded actions and outcomes. The interface stacks the worlds on small screens. Code-first, with design decisions delegated by the user. The simulator and scoring rules determine all results.
